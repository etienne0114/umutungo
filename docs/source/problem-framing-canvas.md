## **A2. Success Definition — Desired Change, Metrics & Failure Boundaries** 

### **1. DESIRED CHANGE** 

### **2. MEASURABLE SUCCESS INDICATORS** 

### **3. DEFINITION OF FAILURE** 

_For the Primary User_ 

_Maintenance officers should be able to identify vehicles and assets that need maintenance early through AIgenerated alerts and recommendations, reducing unexpected breakdowns._ 

##### _For the Institution_ 

_Government institutions should transition from fixed maintenance schedules to data-driven maintenance planning, reducing unnecessary maintenance costs, vehicle downtime, and resource wastage._ 

##### _Systemic Impact_ 

_Improve the efficiency, reliability, and transparency of government asset management through predictive maintenance, extending asset lifespans and ensuring better use of public funds._ 

_Metric 1: Early Detection: Identify at least 80% of assets requiring preventive maintenance before a major failure occurs._ 

_Metric 2: Downtime Reduction: Reduce unexpected vehicle/asset downtime by at least 30% during the pilot. Metric 3: Cost Reduction: Reduce unnecessary or emergency maintenance expenditure by at least 20%. Metric 4 Adoption: Have at least 90% of relevant maintenance and assetmanagement staff actively using the system during the pilot. Metric 5  Data Quality: Achieve at least 90% completeness for key asset, maintenance, and inspection records used by the AI model._ 

_Failure Boundary 1: No Accurate Predictions_ 

_The AI system fails to identify assets at risk of failure early enough to allow preventive maintenance._ 

_Failure Boundary 2:_ 

_No Reduction in Maintenance Costs or Downtime The institution continues experiencing frequent breakdowns, unnecessary maintenance expenses, and prolonged vehicle downtime despite using the AI solution._ 

_Failure Boundary 3: Poor Adoption of the System Maintenance officers and asset managers cannot easily use the system, or they continue relying entirely on the old manual maintenance process._

## **A3. Validated Problem Statement Canvas** 



**FINAL VALIDATED PROBLEM STATEMENT (FINAL SYNTHESIS FOR INCUBATION HANDOFF)** 

**For government asset managers and maintenance officers, the reliance on fixed maintenance schedules and manual inspections makes it difficult to identify vehicles and equipment that need maintenance, resulting in unexpected breakdowns, increased repair costs, and prolonged asset downtime. Success means using an AI-powered predictive maintenance system to identify potential failures early, reduce unnecessary maintenance, minimize unexpected breakdowns, and improve the efficient use of public resources.**
